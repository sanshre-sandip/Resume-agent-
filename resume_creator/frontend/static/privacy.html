Privacy information

Nhyapu Craft runs on the computer where you start it. Resume records are stored in the configured local data folder, and generated documents are stored in the configured output folder. Anyone with access to that computer or those folders may be able to access the files.

Optional AI features

Manual editing, saving, previewing, and exporting do not require AI. If you choose an AI feature, the resume content needed for that request is sent to the AI provider selected in settings. Review the provider’s privacy terms before using it.

Browser storage

The browser may keep assistant history and preferences for this local workspace. You can clear those browser storage entries in your browser settings.
