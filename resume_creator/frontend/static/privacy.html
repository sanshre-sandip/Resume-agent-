PRIVACY INFORMATION

How Nhyapu Craft handles data

This page describes the current Phase 1 implementation. The operator should review and publish jurisdiction-specific privacy terms before public launch.

Account and resume data

The app stores an account email, display name, password hash, and saved resume information in its configured persistent storage. Passwords are not stored as plain text. Resume access is restricted by account ownership checks on the server.

AI features

Creating, editing, saving, previewing, and exporting a resume work without AI. If you enter a provider key, it is held in this browser tab’s session storage and sent to this app’s backend only for a connection test or AI action. The app does not save the key in account files or resume data. For an AI action, the relevant resume content and key are forwarded to your selected provider. Provider keys may incur charges. Review that provider’s terms and privacy information before use. If you are under 18, get parent or guardian permission before sending information to an AI provider or using a provider key.

Cookies and browser storage

The app uses an HTTP-only session cookie and a CSRF token cookie for sign-in and request protection. The layout assistant may keep chat and undo history in this browser. Those browser caches are separate from your saved resume records. Provider keys stay in tab-scoped session storage and are cleared when the tab closes.

Deletion

Account settings includes an action to delete your account, resumes, saved layout suggestions, and generated documents from the app’s active storage. Backups, if configured by the operator, may have a separate retention period.

Contact

For questions about data stored by a deployment, contact that deployment’s operator.
