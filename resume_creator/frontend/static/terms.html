Using Nhyapu Craft

This app runs locally on the computer where it is started. Keep it bound to localhost if you want it accessible only from that computer.

Your resumes

Review your information for accuracy before exporting or sharing a resume. Resume data and generated documents are stored in the folders configured for this app.

Optional AI assistance

AI suggestions can be incomplete or incorrect. Check them carefully. When you use an AI feature, relevant resume content is sent to the provider selected in settings.

Exports

PDF and Word exports depend on the document tools installed on your computer.
