SERVICE INFORMATION

Using Nhyapu Craft

This page summarizes the current Phase 1 service and should be reviewed by the operator before public launch.

Your content

You provide and control the resume information you enter. Review it for accuracy before exporting or using it in an application. The app does not guarantee a job offer or compatibility with every applicant tracking system.

AI assistance

AI features are optional and may be unavailable. AI output can be incomplete or incorrect. Check all suggestions and do not rely on them as a substitute for your own review.

Availability and exports

Resume editing, saving, and PDF export depend on the deployment’s configured storage and document-rendering tools. The service may be unavailable during maintenance or infrastructure problems.

Account security

Use a strong, unique password and sign out when using a shared device. Account settings lets you delete your account and saved resume data from active storage.
