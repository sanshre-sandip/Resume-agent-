GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked
questions.

Is Nhyapu Craft free?

Resume creation, editing, and PDF export are available without a payment flow in this Phase 1 release.

How do I edit my resume?

Open a saved resume in your workspace, edit its sections, and review the live preview. AI-assisted edits are optional and only available when configured.

Can I download a PDF?

Yes. Preview the generated PDF in the workspace, then select Download PDF to save it.

Do I need an AI key?

No. Manual editing, saving, previewing, and exporting work without AI. If you choose to use AI, you can enter an authorized provider key in browser settings or use a key configured by the site operator. Provider keys may incur charges; if you are under 18, ask a parent or guardian before using one.

Where is my resume information stored?

Account and resume data are stored in the app’s configured persistent storage. AI requests send resume text to the configured provider only after you request an AI action and confirm the disclosure.

Can I delete my account and resumes?

Yes. Open Account settings and choose Delete account and resumes. This removes the account and generated resume files from the app’s active storage.
